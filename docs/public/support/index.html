Support

Contact Ruski Report

Emilio Garcia independently operates and supports Ruski Report. Messages are normally answered within three business days.

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Email support

Use the address below for app problems, account or privacy questions, accessibility feedback, moderation questions, and feature feedback.

Email ruskisupport@gmail.com

For a faster response, include the part of the app involved, what you expected, what happened, and any safe steps that reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful when they do not expose private information.

Protect your account

Do not email your password, raw session token, private operator credential, or another person’s private information. Ruski Report support will never ask for a password.

Accounts and privacy requests

Signed-in users can permanently delete their account from Account → Delete Account in the iOS app. This also deletes their comments, credentials, sessions, and block relationships. Email support if the in-app process is unavailable or if you have a question about the privacy policy.

People under 13 may not create an account or post comments. A parent or guardian can use the support email to request deletion of information submitted by a child under 13.

Comment safety

Use Report Comment in the app to send a specific comment to the moderation queue. Use Block User to hide another account’s comments from your signed-in view. Email support for a moderation question or an appeal, and include the match and approximate comment time without copying harmful content into the message.

Reports and support email are not monitored continuously. The normal response commitment is within three business days.

No school affiliation. Ruski Report is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by a school or educational institution. Contact the operator through the email above rather than contacting a school about the app.
